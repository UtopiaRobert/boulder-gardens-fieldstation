Bird House

This is the bird house I usually sleep in.

Ants had gotten in through the cracks in the floor so i put duct tape across the seams. People say in these year round. In the summer the roof opens (as shown) and in winter people have small propane heaters. One of our goals is to make more of these bird houses. So far we have 9 of them. A few months ago I painted and roofed 2 of them with tar paper. Tar paper is rally the way to go but it requires a bit of practice.

While doing the taping i took photographs of all the measurements, in case anyone else wanted to build one in the future.
