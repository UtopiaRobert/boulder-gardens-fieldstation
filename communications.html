Field Report 015 · Communications

Long-range radio across a rugged landscape

Boulder Gardens uses a small LoRa mesh to connect people, vehicles, remote sensors, and solar-powered stations across the sanctuary. It carries low-power messages, location data, and telemetry into terrain where cable and Wi-Fi are impractical.

The network follows a simple rule: run cable where practical, use Wi-Fi where appropriate, and use radio where distance, terrain, or mobility makes it useful.
