This guy came wandering across the road when Josie saw it. Everyone from the community came down to the village to watch. Rick, who is our wildlife expert says it is old, like maybe 80 years old. Robert said that they usually stay within 300 feet (or yards) of their home, so it lived close buy. Dave said (by text) that is was probably a female because it did not have a large horn under it's neck.
Josie followed it down the hill as it went down the road for about 30 minutes.
A first for a lot of us!
