We went to Wild Seeds Community for their first IC (Intentional Communities event.

Zail, Josie and I traveled 3 1/2 hours to attend an event for IC.org. We were all established communities meeting to share contacts and resources. There about 9 or 10 communities all within about an hour's distance. We, from Boulder Gardens came from much farther. We had group introductions, and then breakout groups discussing financing and governance and human resources, then all jumped in the creek and had a communal dinner. We left in the evening before the dancing began

From the IC website:

Founding year
2019
What we do
We are a conscious coliving in Southern California for creative professionals, ecopreneurs, social impact artists, and spiritual activists.
We live on 4.1 acres of rural land, in a beautiful historic property. There are 17 bedrooms, 20 bathrooms, coworking spaces, an art room, a music room, and 3 community kitchens. We also have a tiny home and a mini cabin, with van life and RV hookups. Our event venues include a large grassy lawn and a twinkly patio with an outdoor bar.
We have some edible gardens we are building up and a mini vineyard in our driveway.
We host artist and creative residencies. If you are interested in living here, please fill out this resident application BEFORE EMAILING US.

Our Mission
Help innovators create.
